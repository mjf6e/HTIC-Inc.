Company Overview

A Spin-Off Built on Purpose

HTIC, Inc. is a University of Virginia spin-off biotechnology company dedicated to translating cutting-edge immunology research into transformative therapies for acute and chronic inflammatory diseases.

Our work centers on precision biologics that modulate dysregulated innate immune pathways, with a particular focus on NETosis, an inflammatory mechanism that drives organ injury in sepsis, ARDS, trauma, and autoimmune disorders. Founded on technology developed in Dr. Jianjie Ma's laboratory at UVA and Dr. Yongqing Li's laboratory at the University of Michigan, HTIC holds an exclusive license for the hCitH3-mAb platform and recently received IND clearance from the U.S. FDA, enabling the launch of clinical development.

The Biology

Targeting the Biology of Inflammation

Neutrophil extracellular traps, or NETs, are a natural immune defense. But when NETosis runs unchecked, it becomes a driver of catastrophic organ failure — and citrullinated Histone H3 is the biomarker at the center of that damage.

1

Neutrophils deploy NETs

Web-like traps of DNA and protein are released to catch invading pathogens.

2

NETosis runs unchecked

In sepsis, ARDS, and trauma, the same defense becomes a feedback loop that injures healthy tissue.

3

CitH3 marks the damage

Citrullinated Histone H3 is released as the recognized biomarker of this NET-driven pathology.

4

hCitH3-mAb neutralizes it

Our humanized antibody selectively binds and neutralizes CitH3, interrupting the loop while preserving immune function.
